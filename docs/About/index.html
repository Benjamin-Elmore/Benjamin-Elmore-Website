About

01 / Bio

I am a computer science student at California Baptist University, pursuing a B.S. with a concentration in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence (expected May 2029). Since fall 2025, I have served as a software engineer on CBU1, the university VEX U robotics team, and since October 2026 as Vice President of CBU Robotics.

Most of my work is in embedded C++. My current project is ARC, firmware for the ARSENAL, a custom ESP32-S3 board designed to connect third-party I²C sensors to the VEX V5 platform, so that future teams can add sensors without specialized embedded expertise. Independently, I developed MarketTesting, a Python project covering the machine learning workflow from data acquisition and cleaning to LSTM model training.

I intend to pursue a career in embedded and machine learning software, with particular interest in the defense industry. As a leader, I try to serve the team: I show up consistently, check in regularly and help team members directly.
