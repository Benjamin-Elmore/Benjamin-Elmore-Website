About

Bio

I’m Benjamin Elmore, a computer science student at California Baptist University, where I’m concentrating in machine learning and artificial intelligence. I work on embedded software for robots: C++ firmware where sensors, a custom board and a robot controller meet. On the CBU1 VEX U Robotics team I contribute to ARC, the sensor firmware for a custom ESP32-S3 board that connects external sensors to a VEX V5 Brain, and I’m the team’s Vice President.

I started on the team in September 2025 writing PROS/C++ drivetrain code: driver controls, point navigation from odometry, and early PID and Pure Pursuit experiments. That work led me toward the hardware side. Now I spend more time on device interfaces, bus ownership and diagnostics, and on checks someone else can repeat.

I want to keep working on embedded software for physical systems, and I’m especially interested in defense and space. Sensor interfaces for test fixtures and manufacturing equipment are an area I want to grow into. Outside of robotics, I build smaller Python projects such as MarketTesting, a TensorFlow learning project.
