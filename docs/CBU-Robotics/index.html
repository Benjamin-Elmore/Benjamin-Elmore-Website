Responsible for coordinating milestones across the discipline teams, supporting the planning of events and competitions, and, more recently, supporting the team's weekly Responsible Engineer updates.

  • Coordinate engineering milestones across the build, electronics and software sub-teams and support engineers in meeting deadlines.
  • Support planning of team events, competitions and the team's participation in the VEX U World Championship.

Software Engineer

Since September 2025

C++ development for the competition robots on PROS and for ARC on ESP-IDF. Focus has progressed from robot control to communication between sensors, the team's custom board and the V5 Brain.

Drivetrain and navigation, fall 2025

  • Developed driver controls, a PID controller and point-to-point navigation that converts odometry into left, right and strafe motor commands.
  • Prototyped Pure Pursuit path following with spline search; spline generation and odometry integration remained open at the transition to ARC.

ARC sensor firmware, from August 2026

  • Develop ARC, the C++ firmware for ARSENAL, the team's custom ESP32-S3 sensor board. See the ARC case study.
  • Implemented its sensor abstraction, shared I²C bus management and RS-485 protocol.
  • Verified RS-485 parsing of V5 Brain test packets on the bench in October 2026; sensor reads and full-pipeline hardware verification are the next steps.

Tools: C++, PROS, ESP-IDF, FreeRTOS, PlatformIO, ESP32-S3, I²C, UART, RS-485, Git
